01 — What it is

Not a script. Your knowledge, in a chat.

We build a chat agent on your documents, product information and policies. It answers the questions your team gets every day, says when it doesn’t know, and passes the conversation to a person with the context attached.

  • 01

    Grounded in your sources

    Answers come from the material you approve: FAQs, policies, product sheets, manuals.

  • 02

    Knows when to hand over

    Clear rules for what it answers, what it doesn’t, and when a person takes over.

  • 03

    Connected where it helps

    Where your systems allow it, it can look things up, like an order or a booking. We decide together what it may do.

  • 04

    Kept up to date

    We review conversations, fill the gaps in its knowledge and adjust the rules.

02 — Who it’s for

For teams that answer the same questions every day.

E-commerce and DTC brands

Shipping, returns, sizing and product questions, answered on your site.

Service companies

Questions about services, availability and how to get started, before a person steps in.

Internal teams

An assistant on your internal knowledge, so colleagues find answers without asking around.

03 — How it works

From your FAQ to a live agent.

  1. 01

    Collect

    The questions your team answers most, and the documents that answer them.

  2. 02

    Build

    Knowledge base, tone of voice, answer rules and handover.

  3. 03

    Test

    On real past questions, with your team, before it goes live.

  4. 04

    Launch and improve

    Live on your channel; we review conversations and keep it current.

04 — Deliverables

What you get. Concrete, and yours to keep.

  • Chat agent on your website
  • Knowledge base from your documents
  • Tone of voice and answer rules
  • Handover to a person, with context
  • Test set built from real questions
  • Team training and written playbook
