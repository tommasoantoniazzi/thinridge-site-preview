01 — What it is

Not a script. Your business, in a chat.

We set up an assistant on your own information: products, services, prices, policies and frequently asked questions. It answers the questions your team gets every day, says when it doesn’t know, and hands the conversation to a person with a summary attached.

  • 01

    Grounded in your information

    Answers come from the material you approve, kept up to date as your business changes.

  • 02

    Your tone of voice

    Formal or friendly, written the way your business talks to customers.

  • 03

    Knows when to hand over

    Complaints, delicate requests and anything it can’t answer reach your team, with the conversation summarised.

  • 04

    Clear summaries

    You see what customers ask, what was handled and what was passed to the team.
