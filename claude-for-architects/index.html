01 — What it is

Less time on documents. More time on design.

Architecture runs on text as much as on drawings: briefs, regulations, reports, specifications, emails, competition entries. We show your team how to hand that work to Claude, check what it returns, and fit it into how the studio already works.

  • 01

    Read and summarise

    Client briefs, building regulations, technical standards and tender documents, with references you can check.

  • 02

    Write in your studio’s voice

    Project reports, competition texts, specifications and client emails, as drafts your team reviews.

  • 03

    Organise every job

    A Claude Project for each job, with the studio’s documents and standing instructions.

  • 04

    Work from images and PDFs

    Describe, compare and extract information from site photos, references and drawings you share.

05 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Every project is tailored to the company. After a first call we send a fixed proposal with scope, timeline and price.

Book a call
Do we need technical skills?

No. If your team writes emails and reads PDFs, they can learn this. We start from the work you already do.

Can Claude make renders or drawings?

Claude works with the text, documents and images you give it. It doesn’t replace your CAD, BIM or rendering software; we focus on the work around the drawings.

What about confidential project files?

We agree together which documents to use, and set up the studio’s accounts and rules accordingly.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

It depends on the size of the team and what you want to cover. After a first call we send a fixed proposal.
