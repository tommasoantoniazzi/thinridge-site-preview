01 — What it is

Structure the work around the model. Faster, and checked.

Much of engineering time goes into preparing data, scripting repetitive steps, writing reports and checking numbers. We show your team how to use Claude for that work, with clear rules on what it may produce and how it gets verified.

  • 01

    Scripting and automation

    Scripts and automations for the modelling and analysis tools your team already uses.

  • 02

    Data structuring

    Turn spreadsheets, schedules and model exports into clean tables, and spot what’s missing.

  • 03

    Reports and documentation

    Drafts of calculation reports, method statements and technical notes, built from your results.

  • 04

    Review and consistency checks

    Cross-checking inputs, units and consistency, as support to engineering verification, never a replacement.

Does Claude do the structural calculations?

No. Claude helps write scripts, organise data and draft reports. Calculations run in your validated software and are checked by a qualified engineer.

Which software does it work with?

We start from the tools your team already uses and look at them together in the first call.

Do we need to know how to code?

No. Claude writes the scripts; we teach your team how to ask for them, read them and test them.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?

Format, duration and price are tailored to your team and defined together in the proposal after a first call.
